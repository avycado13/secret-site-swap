good questions

faq

do i need to be good at coding?

Nope. Start with a single HTML page and make it your own. Small and handmade is the point.

what should my site be about?

Anything you want! Tell a story, make a toy, collect your favorite things, or try the idea generator above.

does it need to be finished?

It doesn't need to be huge or perfect. Give your visitor something delightful to find, then share it when you're ready.

how do i share my site?

Publish your page somewhere it can be opened with a link, then trade that link with another Hack Clubber.
